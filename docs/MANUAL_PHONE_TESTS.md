# Manual Phone Tests

1. Create a patient and copy the family key.
2. Connect a caregiver and check the count on both dashboards.
3. Add one medicine with two future times.
4. Close the patient app and verify the scheduled alarm appears.
5. Tap Snooze and verify it alarms again after ten minutes.
6. Mark the medicine taken and check the caregiver update.
7. Send a caregiver reminder while the patient app is closed.
8. Edit, delete and reset medicine.
9. Update patient and caregiver profiles.
10. Switch to Bangla and inspect every screen.
11. Switch back to English.
12. Check the launcher icon, notification icon, alarm pill and medicine-name visibility.

Record the phone model, Android version, test date and whether every step passed.
